The purpose, the proposal, and how to take part.

What is the Framers Linguistic Protocol?

FLP is an open-source legal clarity initiative exploring how digital tools and public participation can make legal language more transparent, precise, and understandable.

Why is legal clarity important?

Clear language helps citizens understand, follow, and challenge laws. Ambiguous wording makes consistent interpretation and accountability harder.

Is FLP tied to a political party?

The existing project FAQ describes FLP as independent of any party and open to citizens who value transparency, legal precision, and individual liberty.

How does FLP relate to AI?

AI-assisted analysis is a proposed tool for identifying ambiguous language. The project does not currently provide a working legal analysis service on this website.

Is there a cryptocurrency involved?

The project proposes a token system with the ticker FRA for participation and governance. This website does not verify a launched token or offer a way to buy one.

How can I get involved?

Read the protocol, review the project on GitHub, or send feedback using the form below.

Where is the white paper?
