Research and development · Proposed capabilities

Clarity first

FLP proposes combining linguistic analysis, public feedback, and transparent records to identify ambiguous language and support clearer alternatives.

  1. Identify the source

    Start with the law or policy text and its jurisdiction. Preserve the original wording so interpretations remain traceable.

  2. Examine the language

    Highlight ambiguity, contradictory definitions, and differences across state-level legal templates.

  3. Invite public review

    Discuss interpretations and proposed revisions in the open, with clear reasoning and source references.

  4. Record the proposal

    Explore cryptographic validation and versioned records to make changes and their rationale easier to inspect.
