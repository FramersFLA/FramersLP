Freedom Through Clarity

A cryptographic framework for legal transparency, unifying the states under constitutional clarity.

Why FLP?

Framers Linguistic Protocol is an open-source initiative that empowers citizens to challenge vague or manipulative legal language by leveraging blockchain transparency and collective interpretation.

How it Works

Using cryptographic validation and cross-state legal templates, FLP brings uniformity and citizen input into a decentralized legal protocol built for modern governance.
