About Framers Linguistic Protocol

The Framers Linguistic Protocol (FLP) is an open-source legal clarity initiative designed to restore transparency, consistency, and citizen agency in how law is written, interpreted, and enforced in the United States.

Born out of concern over state-level overreach and vague legal language, FLP aims to create a new legal and digital framework using decentralized tools like Ocean Protocol and token-based access controls.

Our protocol integrates smart contracts, linguistic analysis, and public feedback to ensure laws and policies adhere to constitutional values while remaining adaptable to modern society.
