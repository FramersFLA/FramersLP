About the Framers Linguistic Protocol

Our Mission

The Framers Linguistic Protocol (FLP) is a civic cryptographic initiative aimed at clarifying legal language and dismantling coercive or manipulative governance through transparency, precision, and open-source tools.

The Problem

Too often, legal documents are written in language that is intentionally vague or overly complex. This ambiguity can obscure meaning, hide coercion, or limit public understanding of the laws that govern them.

Our Solution

FLP leverages cryptographic tools, decentralized collaboration, and emerging AI-assisted language models to expose and reform unclear legal language. The protocol enables citizens to flag, analyze, and challenge problematic clauses at local, state, and national levels.

Who It's For

FLP is designed for everyone — engaged citizens, grassroots movements, independent researchers, and technologists — anyone who believes in government by the people and the need for clarity in governance.

Our Values

FLP is open-source, nonpartisan, and community-led. We believe in transparency, fairness, and accountability — and that the tools of tomorrow should serve the public good today.
