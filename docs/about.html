Our mission

The Framers Linguistic Protocol (FLP) is a movement and framework designed to reestablish constitutional clarity and legal precision in modern governance. Built on open-source technology and the foundational language of the U.S. Constitution, FLP empowers citizens to expose vague, contradictory, or exploitative legal language. Our mission is to restore the supremacy of law that reflects the will of the people — not the convenience of bureaucracy or corporate influence.

Built for public understanding

The initiative grew from concern about vague legal language, state-level overreach, and the distance between citizens and the systems that govern them. It brings together legal clarity, open-source collaboration, and digital governance research.

An open framework in development

FLP explores linguistic analysis, cross-state comparisons, decentralized tools such as Ocean Protocol, and token-based access controls. These are proposed directions; this website does not establish that a production protocol or token has launched.
