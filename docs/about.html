About Framers Linguistic Protocol

The Framers Linguistic Protocol (FLP) is a civic technology initiative designed to restore clarity, transparency, and accessibility in American legal and governmental language. Our mission is to build a constitutional framework that reflects the original intent of the framers while using modern technologies like blockchain and AI to prevent linguistic manipulation and uphold civil liberties.

FLP was born from a belief that laws should serve the people first—not obfuscate their rights. Through community governance, transparent data-sharing models, and state-by-state legal harmonization tools, we aim to return power to citizens and ensure state governments uphold the Constitution.
