About Framers Linguistic Protocol

The Framers Linguistic Protocol (FLP) is a movement and framework designed to reestablish constitutional clarity and legal precision in modern governance. Built on open-source technology and the foundational language of the U.S. Constitution, FLP empowers citizens to expose vague, contradictory, or exploitative legal language. Our mission is to restore the supremacy of law that reflects the will of the people — not the convenience of bureaucracy or corporate influence.
