About the Framers Linguistic Protocol

The Framers Linguistic Protocol (FLP) is a citizen-driven initiative designed to restore transparency, clarity, and accountability to the American legal system. Rooted in the original principles of the U.S. Constitution, FLP aims to reduce legal ambiguity by using decentralized technologies and cryptographic verification to track, revise, and standardize legislative language.

With a multi-chain blockchain infrastructure and a tokenized governance model, FLP empowers Americans to audit and propose language improvements to existing laws, exposing deceptive or vague terms while promoting consistent terminology across jurisdictions.

We believe that the foundation of freedom is built on clear understanding. FLP gives voice to citizens, enables technological tools for reform, and defends against abuses by both state and federal overreach through legal clarity and verifiable public consensus.
