Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Framers Linguistic Protocol?

The FLP is a legal and digital framework aimed at restoring clarity and constitutional intent to legal systems using modern open-source tools.

Why is legal clarity so important?

Clarity ensures that citizens can understand, follow, and challenge laws fairly. Vague language opens doors to abuse or exploitation.

What does FLP stand for?

Framers Linguistic Protocol. It represents a return to clear, constitutional language in lawmaking and legal interpretation.

Is FLP a political movement?

FLP is not tied to any party. It’s for citizens who value transparency, legal precision, and individual liberty.

How can I get involved?

Follow us on social media, join the conversation, contribute to our open-source codebase, and spread awareness.

How does FLP relate to AI?

FLP supports using AI to detect and correct ambiguous or exploitative legal language, ensuring justice aligns with original intent.

Is there a cryptocurrency involved?

Yes, our protocol is tied to a token system (ticker FRA) to provide incentives for participation and governance transparency.

Can I submit questions?

Absolutely! Submit questions via the contact page or through our social platforms — a submission box will be added soon.
