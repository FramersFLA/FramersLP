Frequently Asked Questions

What is FLP?

The Framers Linguistic Protocol is a platform for legal language clarity and conformity across U.S. states, built on open-source values and blockchain protocols.

What is the purpose of the project?

To eliminate vague and deceptive legal terminology and enforce constitutional compliance through standardized templates and community participation.

Is FLP a political movement?

FLP is a non-partisan legal framework project intended to empower all Americans, regardless of ideology, through transparency and lawful reform.

Will this impact state sovereignty?

It reinforces federal supremacy while helping states align with constitutional principles using a voluntary legal protocol framework.

How does blockchain fit into FLP?

We use blockchain tools like Ocean Protocol to tokenize access to legal datasets, track versioning, and restrict rogue or abusive AI access.

Who can participate?

Developers, constitutionalists, attorneys, coders, and concerned citizens. It's fully open-source and structured to encourage distributed input.

Can I suggest legal language for reform?

Yes. We welcome user-submitted interpretations, revisions, or breakdowns of complex statutes that can be peer-reviewed and integrated into state-specific templates.

Will there be a native token?

Yes. The FRA ("Framers") token will be used to govern access to data, reward contributors, and ensure participation in protocol decisions.

How does this help stop rogue AI?

By requiring AI models to "pay-to-query" or authenticate via compute-to-data mechanisms, it limits unchecked scraping and manipulation of law-based information.
