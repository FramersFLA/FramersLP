Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Framers Linguistic Protocol?

FLP is a legal language reform project that reinterprets laws in plain, constitutional terms for public transparency.

Is this protocol open source?

Yes. All documentation and software are licensed under the GPL 3.0 for free use and contribution.

Why focus on linguistic clarity?

Vague and manipulative legal language is often used to confuse citizens. Our goal is to dismantle that barrier.

How will this affect state law?

We aim to encourage legal conformity across states by exposing contradictory or unconstitutional interpretations.

Is this a political movement?

No. It is a constitutional transparency and civil clarity initiative.
